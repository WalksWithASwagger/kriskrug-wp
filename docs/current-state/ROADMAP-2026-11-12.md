## Direction (Kris, 2026-11-12)

The site is the professional front door: speaking, work, writing and the public
record behind BC + AI, Vancouver AI and Futureproof. For January to March 2027
it is tuned for one outcome first: **paid keynote bookings**. Kris set four
rulings for this quarter:

- **Paid keynotes lead.** Speaking proof, the signature talk in readable form
  and a clean inquiry path come before new page work.
- **KRÜG becomes a monthly letter, starting in December.** Every signup button
  already points there, so the site must stop promising a weekly note.
- **Open Studio is parked until April.** #977 and #983 keep their prepared
  work; nothing ships or recruits before then.
- **Events stays true.** The weekly proposal sweep returns as a Codex
  automation that Kris owns (#1102), once he approves creating it.
