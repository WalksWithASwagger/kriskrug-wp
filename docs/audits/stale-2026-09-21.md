   `/…` routes, and `https:` URLs. **0 missing targets.**

   basename against `Makefile`, `docs/`, `scripts/`, `.github/`,
   `content/`, and `fixes/`. Flagged one-shot apply helpers whose issues the
   front door already marks closed, plus scripts with zero or test-only
   references.

   `TODO`/`FIXME` tied to issue numbers. No production hits (the only

   `CONTRIBUTING.md`. It does **not** scan `issues-to-create/` or
