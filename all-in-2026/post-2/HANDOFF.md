## 9. Voice & style notes (from post 1)

Match https://kriskrug.co/2026/09/15/headed-east-for-all-in-montreal/ (~2,500 words):
- First person, confessional, self-deprecating beats ("Classic Krüg fieldwork"; "Humbling. One minute I am interrogating the operating conditions for national technological independence. The next minute I cannot independently operate myself onto an airplane.")
