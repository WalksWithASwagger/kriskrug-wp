## Series diagnosis

Article 1 establishes the mission and vocabulary: the wrong boarding line; sovereign AI must be more than vibes; Milton Lim's upstairs/downstairs image; rooms as social systems; curiosity in one hand and critique in the other; BC lacks a national institutional anchor; Kris goes east to connect BC to the national effort and bring the country into the Futureproof room.

Article 2 tests the stance against reality: champions versus saplings; LawZero's $300 million and Solomon upstairs; chalk, vandalism and protest outside; Angelica Lim's mirror; Jeff Ward's boundaries and Indigenous data sovereignty; Hanna Demoz/Amii and BC's partnership mission; lanyards crossing between worlds.

Article 3 cannot rediscover `both hands full` or merely repeat upstairs/downstairs. Those are inherited grammar. The climax is what Kris did with the position: he carried the street's accountability question into institutional rooms and BC's grassroots work toward national power, coming away with relationships and routes forward.

## Selected structure: Two Laptops, One Consultation Kit

**Thesis:** The backpack comedy turns the insider-outsider frame into action. The bag literally carries the tools, evidence, invitations and unfinished work BC brings into Canada's AI rooms.

1. **Cold open: the bag.** During a Vancouver emergency-response presentation, Kris's two-laptop backpack vanishes. Security confiscated it and called the bomb squad. Alexis, who had checked Kris's media credentials the day before, returns it. Their shared laugh becomes how they get to know each other.
2. **Rewind: Martin and the intellectual ballast.** Sept. 16 night, plantains/deviled eggs/pretzels with Martin Lopatka of EPAM. Middle-power AI, sovereign/on-prem infrastructure, Cohere/Aleph Alpha, open-source applied research in BC, and frustration with fake collaboration that is really a sponsorship pitch.
3. **LawZero hinge.** Kris bumps into Neil and Loki Jorgenson outside LawZero, then grabs Justine Gauthier for a five-minute interview before lunch. This answers questions left open by article 2: the Canada-Germany funding goes to talent and compute; LawZero wants safe-by-design systems and a smaller scientist-AI guardrail model. More important, Kris explicitly connects being inside with Solomon and outside with protesters, refusing the us-versus-them split.
4. **The bag's contents become the motif.** Two laptops, recorder, consultation response, reusable facilitation kit, Futureproof invitations, photographs, business cards and promises.
5. **BC's work moves up the chain.** Invest Ottawa; the AI Ethical Futures Lab's formal transparency response and kit; Team BC at Mila; Rick Glumac and Cam making introductions; an ADM route; the Israel/Synhart interview; Mozilla/open-weight safety debate; Martin opening two minutes in the hallway with Evan Solomon.
6. **Political climax: the saplings have arrived.** Article 2 asked who waters saplings. Now a BC sapling arrives with roughly 350 members, a published method, an MP forwarding its consultation response, and a case for a fourth federal AI centre. Access is not the prize. Getting the work and the ask into the room is.
7. **Coda: Alberic in the park.** Alberic Maillet of moonbus, a MUTEK board member, comes to Kris off-program. They jam on creative ecosystems, Quebec's mandate to show regional creativity, MUTEK expansion to Tokyo and possible West Coast expansion, BC AI as delivery partner, and a Futureproof invitation. The official conference ends; ecosystem diplomacy keeps going.

Keep the unnamed institutional contrast implicit. Show the beauty of BC through independent nodes doing work, making introductions and connecting outward. Do not name or attack AInBC. Participatory practice and open doors should simply feel more legitimate than gatekeeping.
