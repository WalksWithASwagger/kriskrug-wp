## Discussions on Canada's AI Landscape and Strategy

This section focuses on high-level discussions regarding Canada's AI strategy, regional economic development, and data governance. A major point of conversation was the C$300 million funding announcement for Yoshua Bengio's AI safety nonprofit, LAZIRA, with funds earmarked for talent and compute. LAZIRA aims to develop "safe-by-design" AI models to act as guardrails for existing systems.

The dialogue also addressed significant challenges within British Columbia's tech landscape. Participants noted BC's restrictive 200-megawatt annual cap on hydropower for data centers and a difficult application process requiring large upfront deposits, which disadvantages startups. A broader strategic concern was raised that BC is being positioned for resource extraction (e.g., aluminum, LNG), while research and innovation are concentrated in other hubs like Montreal. This transactional and monopolistic approach to resource allocation is seen as stifling the local AI ecosystem and leaving a narrative vacuum that could lead to public backlash. Separately, the discussion highlighted the work of Animiki's "Niwin" platform, which supports Indigenous data sovereignty by enabling communities to govern their data according to their own cultural protocols, emphasizing that AI governance is impossible without data governance.
