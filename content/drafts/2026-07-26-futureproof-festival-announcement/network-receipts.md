## Event families

### 1. Northern Voice — Vancouver blogging/social-software unconference, 2005-2013

- **Relationship:** `organized`. Kris Krug is listed by name as one of the conference organizers, alongside Darren Barefoot, Brian Lamb, Boris Mann, and others.
- **Canonical public URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Voice
- **Representative image URL:** none suitable (Wikipedia entry has no Kris-credited photo; his Flickr does not have a standalone public Northern Voice album easy to isolate from the general photostream).
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** This is the earliest and most direct precedent for the Vancouver AI meetups → BC + AI → Futureproof arc. Before he organized anything AI-related, he co-organized a grassroots Vancouver tech/culture conference for years. "I've built a scene into a conference before" is a true claim, not a metaphor.
- **Corroboration strength:** Independent (Wikipedia, third-party-edited).

### 2. Vancouver 2010 Winter Olympics — True North Media House / W2 Culture + Media House

- **Relationship:** `worked for` / `organized`, specifically as **co-founder** of True North Media House, an independent/citizen-media hub built alongside the official Olympic accreditation system, plus contributing photo essays to MediaShift during the Games. This is distinct from and stronger than "photographed the Olympics" — he helped build alternative infrastructure at a mega-event, not just document it.
- **Canonical public URL:** https://mediashift.org/2010/02/true-north-media-house-w2-provide-citizen-media-hub-at-olympics053/
- **Representative image URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/collections/72157623159875205/ (Vancouver 2010 Olympics collection — includes a "True North Media House Accreditation" set and a "W2 Culture + Media House Ribbon Cutting" set)
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** The single strongest lineage receipt for Futureproof's own "non-extractive," "artists have power in the conversation," "the people affected get a voice" language. He has already built a room that gave independent/citizen voices a seat at a mega-event most people only experience from press row.
- **Corroboration strength:** Independent (MediaShift, contemporaneous 2010 journalism).

### 3. PopTech — Camden, Maine, multi-year (2008 through at least 2013)

- **Relationship:** `photographed`, multi-year and credited by the organization's own account. PopTech's official Flickr account captions individual photos "Photo by Kris Krug" (confirmed on 2009 and 2013 images).
- **Canonical public URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/albums/72157608995273506/ (PopTech 2008 album, pre-identified in #643)
- **Representative image URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/poptech/10468089914 (PopTech's own account crediting a 2013 photo to Kris Krug)
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** Years of embedded documentation of a conference built explicitly to cross science, art, and social change in one room — an early, ongoing model for "both hands full" programming.
- **Corroboration strength:** Independent (credited on PopTech's own official account, not just Kris's).
- **Flag:** The 2008 album itself is narrow (13 photos of one performer, "Abdominal"). Use it as the linkable canonical URL per #643's pre-identified list, but do not describe it in article copy as if it were the whole PopTech relationship — the org-credit evidence is what carries the multi-year claim, not this one album's photo count.

### 4. TED / TEDx — TEDxSummit 2012 (Morocco), plus TEDxVancouver, TEDxOilSpill, TEDxShanghai, TEDxHonolulu

- **Relationship:** `photographed`, named and quoted by TED's own editorial team. The official TED Blog (2012) describes him as "one of our photographers and a longtime friend of TEDx" in a post specifically about his TEDxSummit work.
- **Canonical public URL:** https://blog.ted.com/what-does-tedx-mean-to-me-answering-tedxsummit-photographer-kris-krug/
- **Representative image URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/albums/72157630051606048/ (TED@Vancouver Global Talent Search, pre-identified in #643)
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** A single talk format that travels across cities and cultures while keeping its throughline — direct parallel to taking a Vancouver meetup format and scaling it into a festival without losing what made the room work.
- **Corroboration strength:** Independent (TED's own editorial blog, not self-published).
- **Flag:** His own about page lists "TEDxOilSpill" specifically, not the broader roster; the TED Blog post is about TEDxSummit specifically. Do not merge these into an undifferentiated "photographed TED" claim — name the specific TEDx events the public record actually supports.

### 5. SXSW Interactive — Austin, Texas, 2013

- **Relationship:** `photographed`. 801 photos in a single public album documenting venues, attendees, and keynotes (that year's keynote was Elon Musk).
- **Canonical public URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/albums/72157649379627911/ (pre-identified in #643)
- **Representative image URL:** same album (no single standout frame independently verified; use album cover per WP editorial choice in #644).
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** Scale without losing texture — documenting a 30,000-person tech festival while still finding the room-level, human moments, which is the exact needle Futureproof is trying to thread at festival scale.
- **Corroboration strength:** Self-published (his own Flickr), but the photo count and comprehensiveness are independently visible/verifiable on the platform itself.

### 6. DENT (Dent the Future) — Sun Valley, Idaho (2014-2018), Napa Valley (2018), plus a 2023 return

- **Relationship:** Self-attested `official photographer` — his own current about page and a 2023 first-person post both state "DENT's Official Photographer since 2014." This is not independently corroborated (DENT's own site was not reachable for cross-check in this research pass, and DENT's Wikipedia entry does not mention him).
- **Canonical public URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/collections/72157691620626391/ (six-year DENT collection, 2014-2018)
- **Representative image URL:** https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/albums/72157678301027093/ (DENT 2017, pre-identified in #643)
- **Editorial lesson it supports:** This is the most emotionally direct precedent for Futureproof's own philosophy. In his own words (2023): "DENT is more than a conference; it's a part of my life," and he describes it as a "family reunion" where "creativity meets community, and ideas take flight." That is close to verbatim the case Futureproof is making.
- **Corroboration strength:** Self-published only. **Flag per #643 instructions:** treat "Official Photographer since 2014" as Kris's own stated credential, not an independently verified title, when it appears in the article.

- Name specific, linkable events (this ledger), not a general decade-long claim.
- Introduce Northern Voice and True North Media House as the closest direct precedents for "I have built a scene into a founding event before" — material the live post never uses.
- Use the receipts to invite the network in (bat signal), not to justify Kris's credibility in the abstract.
