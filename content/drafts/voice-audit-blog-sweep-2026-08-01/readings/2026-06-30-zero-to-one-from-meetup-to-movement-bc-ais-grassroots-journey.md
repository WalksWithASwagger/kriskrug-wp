## Checker-gap candidates

- **Inflected banned words dodge word boundaries:** "empowering" (`empower`), "fostered" (`foster`) sailed through. Propose adding inflections or stem-matching in `anti-glossary.md` (e.g. `empower(s|ed|ing)?`, `foster(s|ed|ing)?` as regex entries).
- **Uncontracted redefinition-reveal:** "is not just X but Y" / "weren't X but Y" / "This was X, not Y" all dodge the `it'?s not just` and `not only... but also` regexes. Candidate regex: `\b(is|are|was|were)n'?t (just|only|simply) ` and `\bnot just \w+([^.]{0,40}) but `.
- **Third-person self-reference:** `(As )?Krüg (stated|said|notes|explains)` as a soft flag — on this site, Kris quoted in his own prose usually means a pipeline wrote it.
- **Copula avoidance:** soft-flag `\b(serves|stands|stood) as\b`.
- **"proved" tic:** soft-flag `\bproved? (pivotal|crucial|perfect|remarkably)\b`.
