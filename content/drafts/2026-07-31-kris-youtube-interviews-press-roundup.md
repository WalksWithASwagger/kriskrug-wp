### Recent Video Interviews and Talks

These three videos hit the same live wire from different angles: who controls the infrastructure, what happens to creative work, and how we keep the human parts from getting optimized out while AI kicks down the door.

- **[Canada's AI Power Struggle: Energy, Data Centres & Sovereignty | Kris Krüg](https://youtu.be/n_aGBFGnPzo):** [Power Struggle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UzJfMJzkFwc) host Stewart Muir pulls the conversation toward electricity, data centres, Indigenous leadership, national sovereignty, and who gets to shape Canada's AI future.
- **[AI Is Kicking Down the Door: Creativity, Jobs & BC's Future | Kris Krüg](https://youtu.be/Vbk2B7aqw8E):** Recorded at [LLLSummit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8CvTTSWqj8), this talk moves from assistants, automations, and agents into AI literacy, creative disruption, environmental cost, and the choices British Columbia still gets to make.
- **[Live With Curiosity: AI, Creativity & Staying Human | Kris Krüg on Human Biography](https://youtu.be/TOk2YwViBKs):** [Sharad Kharé](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF1taMiIV8Q) and I get into photography, creative practice, AI ethics, and the stubbornly human value of curiosity. [Read the companion article](https://kriskrug.co/2025/01/25/human-biography-podcast-w-sharad-khare/).
