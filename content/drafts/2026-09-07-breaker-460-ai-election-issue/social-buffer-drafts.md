## LinkedIn

Bob Mackin caught me in the parking lot outside the planetarium and asked whether AI would be an issue in this fall's civic election.

I said it would probably be the main one. Council had already made that true. They punted the Telus and Westbank data centre file past the October 17 vote, which means nobody has to vote on it during the campaign and everybody gets to campaign on it.

Two days after the episode ran, The Tyee published where all seven parties stand on AI. Moratorium, framework first, conditional approval, or a mayor on record liking the project. Take your pick, but you have to pick.

Here is the part that bugs me. Nobody from the AI community is quoted in that coverage. The anti-data-centre story and the AI-ecosystem story are still running as two separate beats with two separate casts. That gap is the exact thing the meetup exists to close, and we have not closed it.

23 minutes with Bob and with Dave Olson, who came in from Okayama and stole the episode.
