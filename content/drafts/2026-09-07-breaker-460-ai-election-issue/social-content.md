## LinkedIn

Bob Mackin caught me in the parking lot outside the planetarium and asked whether AI would be an issue in this fall's civic elections.

It already is. Council sent the Telus and Westbank data centre proposals back to city planners, which means nobody has to vote on it during the campaign and everybody gets to talk about it. That is how a zoning file becomes a proxy for who the city is for.

I am less worried about deepfakes than about a room where nobody who is angry bothered to show up.

23 minutes with me and Dave Olson, in from Okayama, on theBreaker.news Podcast edition 460. Dave is the one worth your time. He does not want AI to write his poems. He wants it to fix 5,600 broken links and then handle the mundane.
