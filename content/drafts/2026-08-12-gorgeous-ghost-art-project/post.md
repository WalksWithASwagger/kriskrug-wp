![The jeweled Gorgeous Ghost title and lightning-bolt eye in a field of stars](https://gorgeousghost.com/og/share.jpg)

I made a 69.42-second glam space opera because apparently a normal short film was not enough.

[Gorgeous Ghost](https://gorgeousghost.com/) is live now: a film, a song, a public transcript, and a Signal Atlas that lets you poke around inside the piece after the lights go down. Planet Andromeda speaks in lightning. Civilization is wobbling. The chorus still absolutely slaps.

This is a new art project in the little constellation of things I have been building around AI, music, film, taste, and human judgment. It sits somewhere between a music video, a comic-book transmission, and a very short night at the opera.

## The ghost is a band

The credits call us The Scallywags: **Kris Krüg, Magenta Rune, Mayumi Rawlings, and Kevin Friel**. I made the film and assembled the signal, but the work is better when it reads as a band rather than a solo demo. That is the useful part of making things in public. The strange little object picks up fingerprints from the people around it.

There are also thanks in the transmission to **Kaoru Yoshihira** and **Floyo.ai**, whose work sits in the wider making story. The project was made for **The BC + AI Film Club Prompt Challenge**, which is exactly the kind of constraint I like: short clock, real people, limited runway, make the thing anyway.

## A song with a body around it

The song came first as a signal. The site gives it a body.

You can watch the film, then open the Atlas and move through seven chapters and 29 moments. There is a transcript for the people who would rather read the spell than chase it through a video player. There is a browser-local Relic tool for making a little artifact from the signal. The features keep the work unfolding after the first watch without burying a 69-second film.

The song is also broadcasting through [Ghost Radio](https://ghost.radio.fm/), my pirate little audio lab. Tune the deck and you will find the Gorgeous Ghost collection alongside the other records, half-finished albums, and strange frequencies. A film should be allowed to leak into the radio.

## The network is the artwork

Gorgeous Ghost now has a home of its own, but it is not an island.

- [Punk Rock AI](https://www.punkrockai.com/) is the argument about taste, theft, refusal, and making with the tools without handing over the wheel.
- [Both Hands Full](https://www.bothhandsfull.com/) is the film and learning portal for holding critique in one hand and curiosity in the other.
- [Ghost Radio](https://ghost.radio.fm/) is the audio lab and broadcast deck.
- [AI Garden’s Signal Atlas](https://kriskrug.ai/network) is the living map of the weird public things I am building.
- [KrisKrug.co Work](https://kriskrug.co/work/) is the narrative hub where the threads meet.

That connective tissue matters. A project can be a film and still have a source trail. A song can be a song and still point to the people who helped it become real. An AI-assisted work can show the machinery without pretending the machinery had the taste.

## The human is still on the wheel

The machine handled parts of the generative middle. It did not decide what the film was, which shots survived, where the song turned, how long the silence should last, or whether the thing had any pulse. Those decisions stayed human. They stayed with the ear, the eye, the edit, and the bullshit detector.

That is the part I keep coming back to. AI is useful when it gives me more surfaces to react against. It is less interesting when it asks to be mistaken for the artist.

So: welcome to the network, Gorgeous Ghost. Come through the door, tune the radio, read the Atlas, and tell me what you would have kept in the cut.
