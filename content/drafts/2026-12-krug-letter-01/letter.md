Every post I wrote in November came back to one question: who gets a say?

## Futureproof: Year One

Futureproof ran October 28 to 30 at the H.R. MacMillan Space Centre. Around 10:30 on opening night I stood outside the Sylvia Hotel and recorded a voice note, trying to get it down while it was fresh. The line I'm still sitting with: "A bunch of people trusted me."

The post is my thank-you and my notes for year two. **Amber Case** brought us back to our bodies. **Jacob Ortiz** put his own project's misses up on the screen, and I respect the hell out of that. **Brewster Kahle** said nope to coming back for another talk, then proposed a small stipend to bring researchers to Vancouver instead. **Michelle Diamond**'s photographs do a lot of the remembering.

[Read Futureproof: Year One](https://kriskrug.co/2026/11/01/futureproof-2026-year-one/)

## You Can't Rent a Social Licence

Three days after Futureproof closed, students brought pots and pans to UBC's AMS Nest to protest an AI summit in their own student building. I photographed it.

Then I wrote about social licence, the public trust that lets this work take root, and how fast a rollout can burn through it. The last section is aimed at me. If I say our community welcomes skepticism, the proof is what we change after we hear it.

[Read You Can't Rent a Social Licence](https://kriskrug.co/2026/11/02/you-cant-rent-a-social-licence/)

## The artists are in QA

On Andrew Callaghan's Channel 5, Grimes asked why there aren't more artists in tech. I know where some of them are. Check QA.

**Suzy Easton** played bass in Minto and Seafoam, recorded with Steve Albini, built guitar pedals out of actual toasters, and has put in more than twelve years in QA and support engineering. On day two of Futureproof she played her solo electronic debut on an AI instrument she's building herself. If you're a musician who keeps getting told to pick a side, come find her and the AI + Music crew at BC + AI. Bring a toaster.

[Read Grimes Asked Where the Artists Are. They're in QA.](https://kriskrug.co/2026/11/08/who-in-the-room-can-hear-the-music/)

## Canada Now, with Ashley Smith

**Ashley Smith** and I go back to the Northern Voice days, and her Canada Now interview was our first real sit-down. I told her about two people from Surrey who came to one of our Vancouver gatherings. They wanted to bring their friends. They also told me what it cost to get in and how long it took to cross the region. Their idea became our first regional chapter, and Fraser Valley AI carries that work now.

We also got into the 1,800 or so of my photographs I found in one dataset, and the people in those frames I never got to ask.

[Read the post](https://kriskrug.co/2026/11/09/who-gets-a-say-canada-ai-future-canada-now/) or [watch the episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vC-zpjQM3E)
