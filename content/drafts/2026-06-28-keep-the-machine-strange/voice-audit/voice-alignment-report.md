## Flagged (judgment calls, quoted)

### J1. Antithesis density — the one real voice risk (judgment call, partially actionable)
The piece's signature move is the Postman-style antithesis: "X is not A. It is B." Instances: the excerpt ("did not predict AI. He left us something more useful"), the meta description ("not rejection. It is civic attention"), pull quote line 45 ("not AI panic. It is civic attention"), pull quote line 61 ("does not need more priests... needs citizens with receipts"), body line 107 ("not only bias testing... It is responsibility tracking"), line 123 ("None of that is anti-AI. It is anti-stupid-AI"), closing pull quote line 188 ("AI is not the weather. It is made by people...").

Seven instances in one essay. Each is individually earned, and antithesis is legitimately Postman's own register, so the density is partly thematic. But this is also the known decontracted-reveal AI cadence, and at seven repetitions a reader with a tuned ear may feel the template. Recommendation: cash in the weakest instance, line 107 body text (rewrite provided in slop-check-findings.md, already verified clean). The pull quotes and the anti-stupid-AI joke earn their slots. Leave the rest.
