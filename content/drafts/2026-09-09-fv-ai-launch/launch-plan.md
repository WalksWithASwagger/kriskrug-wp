# FV+AI September 9 Launch Plan

## Outcome

Launch a credible, community-first Fraser Valley room under the BC + AI banner, with Darren Coleman as the local lead and a format that can become monthly without depending on Kris's physical presence.

## Ownership model

| Area | Owner | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Local host | Darren Coleman | Venue, hospitality, local invite list, room facilitation |
| Ecosystem connector | Kris Krüg / BC + AI | Official banner, Luma, distribution, speaker connections, recap amplification |
| Surrey continuity | Matthew Schwartzman | Share what worked in Surrey; help prevent duplicate or competing community narratives |
| First-room sponsor | Coleman Technologies | Space, pizza, drinks; no sales pitch |
| Capture | Assign before event | Consent check, one group photo, attendance/sign-in, three useful quotes, next-date feedback |

## Audience

- Fraser Valley business owners and operators
- people already applying AI in real workflows
- builders and practitioners who can share what actually works
- curious neighbours who do not identify as AI experts
- selected Surrey community members who can bridge the two rooms

For the pilot, cap approved registrations at 20. Build a waitlist rather than overfilling the office.

## Program frame

The event should answer one useful question: **what are people in the Fraser Valley actually trying to do with AI, and where are they getting stuck?**

Avoid positioning the first event as a cybersecurity, Copilot, or Coleman Technologies seminar. Those can become future topics if the community asks for them.

## Recommended run of show

| Time | Segment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00 PM | Doors, pizza, drinks | Name tags; Darren welcomes people individually |
| 6:25 PM | Opening | Darren: why a local room, why now, why BC + AI; five minutes |
| 6:30 PM | Surrey-to-Fraser-Valley bridge | Matthew shares three things Surrey learned; ten minutes plus questions |
| 6:45 PM | Local practical use case | One business owner, ten minutes plus questions; no product pitch |
| 7:05 PM | Round-the-room pulse | Name, work, one AI question or friction point; 30–45 seconds each |
| 7:25 PM | Open discussion | Darren facilitates around themes surfaced by the room |
| 7:50 PM | Commitments and next date | Ask what the next room should cover; invite two volunteers |
| 8:00 PM | Formal close | Informal conversation can continue briefly if the office allows |

If Matthew cannot attend, replace his segment with a short recorded welcome or a Surrey lessons readout credited to him.

## Success measures

- 15–20 approved attendees with a real mix of operators, practitioners, and curious neighbours
- at least 70% show rate
- no sales pitch or single-vendor framing
- every attendee speaks at least once
- two volunteers or contributors emerge for the second event
- next date and topic direction captured within 24 hours
- recap and thank-you completed within two business days

## Guardrails

- Do not publish the exact venue, guest list, host permissions, or access settings without review.
- Do not use quotes attributed to Kris unless the wording is source-verified and approved for this release.
- Do not claim a confirmed speaker or topic until they say yes.
- Keep Surrey visible in the origin story.
- Use “official regional community within BC + AI,” not a franchise or Darren-owned chapter narrative.
