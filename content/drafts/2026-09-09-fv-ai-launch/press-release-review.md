# Review of Darren's August 10 FV+AI Press Release

> **Archived August 22, 2026.** The release published August 11, and this duplicate review is no longer an active send gate. Use the [canonical kk-kb review](https://github.com/WalksWithASwagger/kk-kb/tree/main/content/projects/02-bc-ai-ecosystem-nonprofit/community-programs/regional-chapters/fraser-valley-ai/press-release-review.md).

**Recommendation:** hold submission until the event link, profile link, time, and ownership language are corrected. The release has a sound regional premise and a usable Darren quote, but it is not yet aligned with the community-first story Darren says he wants.

## Must fix before submission

### 1. Lead with the community, not “Darren Coleman launches”

Current headline:

> Darren Coleman Launches FV+AI, a Fraser Valley Community Within BC's Grassroots AI Ecosystem

Recommended headline:

> FV+AI Brings a New Community-Led AI Gathering to the Fraser Valley

Recommended subhead:

> Built from Surrey AI momentum and connected to the BC + AI Ecosystem, the first FV+AI gathering takes place September 9 in Langley.

This still names Darren prominently in the body while preserving the distributed-credit story.

### 2. Add the correct weekday and time

September 9, 2026 is **Wednesday**, not Tuesday. Add the time only after Darren confirms that the July 6:00–8:00 PM plan carries forward.

### 3. Do not send press to a missing RSVP page

The release currently says details are available at `bc-ai.ca`, but there is no live FV+AI event page. The press release should point directly to the Luma page once it exists.

### 4. Remove or fix the Darren profile link

The release uses `bc-ai.ca/members/darren-coleman`. Darren's profile/login has been an unresolved issue since July, and the legacy profile route currently redirects to the general member directory. Do not publish a broken or generic profile destination.

### 5. Credit Surrey and Matthew

The release skips the direct predecessor. Add a sentence such as:

> FV+AI grows from a series of Surrey AI gatherings led by Matthew Schwartzman and local collaborators, extending that community spirit deeper into the Fraser Valley.

### 6. Narrow unconfirmed program claims

The release says early sessions will focus on AI governance, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, Copilot, and operational resilience. Those may be credible Darren/Coleman topics, but they are not yet an agreed FV+AI program. Replace with:

> Early sessions will be shaped with the community and will centre practical use cases, responsible adoption, local business realities, and honest peer exchange about what is working.

### 7. Clarify ownership

“Founder of FV+AI” is not wrong as a description of Darren's local initiative, but the shared-source record is stronger and less political:

> Darren Coleman, local organizer of FV+AI and Founder and CEO of Coleman Technologies

Use “official regional community within BC + AI,” and avoid language that implies a separate Darren-owned organization.

## Claims that need a final fact check

- BC + AI “launching in 2025” versus the earlier Vancouver AI community history
- “province-wide” if the release needs to enumerate active regions
- “Indigenous knowledge keepers” as a network description, unless BC + AI is comfortable foregrounding that in this specific release
- the exact wording and publication source for “AI is best when it brings people together”
- Forbes, ABC, Harvard, bestseller, and 25+ years claims; Darren's public BC + AI profile supports them, but EIN should still receive source links

## Suggested replacement opening

**LANGLEY, BC — August 12, 2026** — A new community-led AI gathering is taking root in the Fraser Valley. **FV+AI**, an official regional community within the **BC + AI Ecosystem Association**, will bring local business owners, builders, practitioners, and curious neighbours together for practical, responsible conversations about artificial intelligence. The first gathering takes place Wednesday, September 9 in Langley.

FV+AI grows from the momentum of the Surrey AI meetups and from a simple local need: people across Langley, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, and the wider valley want a useful AI room closer to home. **Darren Coleman**, Founder and CEO of Coleman Technologies, is leading the local effort with support from **Kris Krüg**, the BC + AI community, and Surrey organizer **Matthew Schwartzman**.

## Suggested Kris quote for review

Do not publish this as a Kris quote until Kris approves it:

> “The goal is not to build a Vancouver empire. It is to help local people build rooms they can own. Darren brings the Fraser Valley relationships, Matthew and the Surrey crew bring hard-won community experience, and BC + AI brings the connective tissue.”

This is a new draft quote based on the documented operating model, not a verbatim quote from Kris.
