## June 19, 2026 — Kris Krüg and Darren Coleman planning call

**Record type:** reconstructed meeting record, not verbatim  
**Basis:** June 12–22 email thread and Darren's detailed post-call follow-up  
**Participants:** Kris Krüg, Darren Coleman

### Reconstructed discussion

**Darren:** There is local interest in AI from Walnut Grove Toastmasters, business owners, and people who find Vancouver too far. The intention is to complement BC + AI and the Surrey community, not compete with them.

**Kris:** Fraser Valley is exactly where BC + AI should become useful beyond Vancouver. The existing Surrey events create a real foothold. Darren, Kris, and Matthew should shape this together, with enough runway to build it properly.

**Darren:** Coleman Technologies can host a small first gathering at the Langley office. The main room works for presentation and conversation; the boardroom can hold pizza and drinks. Keep it invite-only at roughly 20 people while the format is tested.

**Kris:** Start with the room, not a heavy program. Food and conversation should lead. Use one short anchor and let the people in the room surface the real agenda.

**Darren:** A recurring monthly cadence is possible, but the first night can be a pilot and the later dates can adjust based on what lands.

### Decisions captured by the follow-up

- Pilot venue: Coleman Technologies, Langley
- Pilot size: approximately 20, invite-only
- Food/hospitality: pizza, pop, and water
- Program: short practical input plus conversation
- Relationship: build within BC + AI and connect to Surrey
- Timing principle: six to eight weeks of launch runway

### Open items after the call

- Final date and time
- Official chapter naming and banner
- Speaker/anchor for the first gathering
- Recurring cadence after the pilot
