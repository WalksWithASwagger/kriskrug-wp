# FV+AI Visual Notes

## Cover A — Fraser Valley mural

**File:** `fvai-luma-cover-mural.png`  
**Dimensions:** 1672×941  
**Use:** Luma cover candidate and future chapter identity

The mural follows the existing BC + AI regional visual family while giving Fraser Valley its own place language: river, salmon, farms, suburban Langley, mountains, cedar, and a community table.

Strengths:

- immediately reads as a distinct regional chapter
- strong family resemblance to the Surrey and Comox Valley artwork
- all event copy is legible and inside safe margins
- can become a recurring FV+AI identity beyond the first event

Watch-outs:

- generated community figures are illustrative, not real attendees
- the scene is visually dense at very small sizes

## Cover B — a new room for the Fraser Valley

**File:** `fvai-luma-cover-room.png`  
**Dimensions:** 1672×941  
**Use:** recommended first-event Luma cover

This direction is less mythic and more immediate: pizza, laptops, notebooks, a small table, and the valley outside. It communicates the actual promise of the pilot rather than an abstract technology event.

Strengths:

- simple hierarchy survives Luma card crops
- no invented speaker portraits or sponsor claims
- grounded in Darren's proposed format and venue energy
- copy gives the launch a memorable line without hype

Watch-outs:

- does not carry the full BC + AI logo; host identity must be clear in the Luma host block and description
- “a new room” should stay launch-specific, not become the permanent chapter tagline unless it earns that role

## Recommendation

Use **`fvai-luma-cover-room-square.png`** for the September 9 Luma event. It is a native 1254×1254 square and avoids relying on automatic crop behaviour. Keep **`fvai-luma-cover-mural-square.png`** as the FV+AI chapter graphic and for the broader announcement once the Luma page is live.

The 16:9 versions remain useful for LinkedIn, event listings, and page heroes.
