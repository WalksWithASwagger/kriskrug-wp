## ALL IN 01: Headed East for ALL IN Montréal

**What is ALL IN Montréal?**
ALL IN is Canada's large AI and technology gathering in Montréal. Kris Krüg attended the September 16-17, 2026 edition as accredited media and wrote this dispatch on the road east into the event.

**Who wrote this?**
Kris Krüg: Vancouver-based creative technologist, Executive Director of BC + AI, and founder of Futureproof Festival of AI. He is reporting on a world he is also trying to build.

**Where and when?**
Montréal, Québec. ALL IN ran September 16-17, 2026. This post is the September 15 travel and setup-day dispatch before the doors opened.

**Why does this matter for British Columbia?**

This post connects the Vancouver practice to Canadian and global peers without pretending BC already has a Mila-scale institutional anchor.
