What happened on day one at ALL IN Montréal?

Day one mixed main-stage announcements inside the Palais (including the Cohere and Aleph Alpha merger news, LawZero funding, and a Mila-DFKI partnership) with street-level protest and chalk outside. The post follows people between those floors, not a single press release.

Who is this for?

Builders, artists, and civic folks tracking how Canada's AI narrative shows up in rooms versus on the sidewalk, and anyone following Kris Krüg, BC + AI, or Futureproof.

Where and when?

Palais des congrès area, Montréal. September 16, 2026 (ALL IN day one).

Why do BC + AI and Futureproof care?

Futureproof Festival of AI (Vancouver, Oct 28-30 2026) and BC + AI are designing rooms where curiosity and critique stay in both hands. Day one at ALL IN is a live case of that tension: announcements upstairs, chalk and questions downstairs. Useful context before October in Vancouver.
