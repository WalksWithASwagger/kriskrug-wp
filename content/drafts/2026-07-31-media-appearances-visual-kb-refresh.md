# Media Appearances Visual KB Refresh

## Recent Press and Quoted Commentary

The camera and mic are only half the trail. The work also shows up when reporters need somebody who can connect AI infrastructure, creative labour, public policy, and what people are actually feeling.

- [**Business in Vancouver: B.C. groups push to build a stronger AI ecosystem**](https://www.biv.com/news/technology/bc-groups-push-to-build-a-stronger-ai-ecosystem-12601298) (2026): clean energy, Indigenous-owned data centres, and an AI economy that lands inside every profession.
- [**The Tyee: Who gets a say in AI adoption?**](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/07/24/Who-Gets-Say-AI-Adoption/) (2026): data centres as heavy industry, public bargaining, and consultation that can still change the outcome.
- [**Business in Vancouver: AI-driven shakeups in legal work**](https://www.biv.com/news/economy-law-politics/bc-lawyers-face-ai-driven-shakeups-in-legal-work-12415161) (2026): how legal AI could democratize parts of practice, while training and culture decide what actually gets adopted.
- [**Pique Newsmagazine: We can't abdicate this future**](https://www.piquenewsmagazine.com/local-news/we-cant-abdicate-this-future-to-governments-or-tech-bros-says-ai-expert-10076883) (2025): public agency and refusing to leave the future to governments or tech bros.
- [**CBC News and The Early Edition: Meta, Instagram, and platform power**](https://www.cbc.ca/news/instagram-1.7437667) (2025): with Jovian Radheshwar and Stephen Quinn on how embedded tech has become in the economy and governance.
- [**BC Studies: Building a Grass Roots AI Community of Practice**](https://ojs.library.ubc.ca/index.php/bcstudies/article/view/199875) (2025): co-authored with Patrick Pennefather and David Gaertner, documenting the Vancouver AI Community as a grassroots use case.

For the broader written trail, older interviews, citations, and photography history, see [**Press and Publications**](https://kriskrug.co/publications/).
