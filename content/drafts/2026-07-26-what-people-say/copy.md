Voice: Dark Crystal KK. Direct. No em dashes. No AI throat-clearing. No "fresh proof." No newsletter collision language ("Named people," "dispatch," "field notes," "subscribe").

Target role: **proof**, not email capture.

Primary CTA should point to speaking/contact or testimonials archive, not Beehiiv.
