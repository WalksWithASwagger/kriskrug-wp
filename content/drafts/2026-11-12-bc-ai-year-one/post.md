BC + AI became a registered nonprofit society on August 22, 2025. Five days later we threw the launch party at [Vancouver AI Meetup #20](https://bc-ai.ca/events/vancouver-ai-meetup-2025-08), under the planetarium dome at the H.R. MacMillan Space Centre. My parents were in the audience. Jos Duncan-Asé flew in from Philadelphia. Thirty-four people signed up that night for an association that was five days old.

A year later we're past 300 members.

Our first annual general meeting is coming up. **[KK: the AGM date, place and format, and how members get notice. Open-facts question 6.]** Before members sit down for it, here's the year, receipts first.

*Vancouver AI Meetup #32, August 26, 2026, our first anniversary. It opens on the year, then turns into my [Futureproof](https://kriskrug.co/2026/11/01/futureproof-2026-year-one/) pitch.*

## Zero to 300, in public numbers

Every number in this list is already public on [bc-ai.ca](https://bc-ai.ca/about). I'd rather show you receipts.

- **300+ members of the nonprofit.** People who pay for a [membership](https://bc-ai.ca/membership). Not our Discord, and not everyone who's ever come to a meetup.
- **$340 a year** for an individual, with at least 25% off every BC + AI-produced event.
- **Three regions with a room:** Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Comox Valley.
- **[Four public programs](https://bc-ai.ca/certification):** Responsible AI Professional, the AI Animation & Filmmaking Accelerator, AI Upgrade and Local AI Fundamentals.
- **94+ documented events and 3,000+ in total attendance.** Attendance counts visits, not unique people. The events count starts in 2023, before the nonprofit existed.
- **One festival.** Futureproof ran October 28 to 30 at the Space Centre.

We're member-funded. On the anniversary night I put it this way from the stage:

> I don't sell you to sponsors.

That's the deal, and it's why the next part matters.

## The lesson I paid for

Before there was a nonprofit, the meetup sold a Core AI annual ticket: $450 for a year of events. Those people backed us before there was anything official to back.

When the association launched, membership replaced the ticket. It cost less and came with more. We sent those early supporters voice messages, offered them membership at about $200, and moved their channel into the members' space. The long version is in [Zero to One](https://kriskrug.co/2026/06/30/zero-to-one-from-meetup-to-movement-bc-ais-grassroots-journey/).

**[KK: the renewal story, in your words. What went wrong when the old ticket renewed, what the refunds looked like, and what it taught you. Open-facts question 2.]**

So founding members got one promise from us. **[KK: the rule in your words, for example: you keep the price you joined at for as long as you stay a member. Open-facts question 3.]**

The founding drive closed on December 31, 2025, and some prices have gone up since. A founding student membership was $80. A new one is $115. A five-person team pack was $600. It's $850 now.

Leaving should be as easy as joining. You cancel from your account dashboard, and there are [no retention scripts](https://bc-ai.ca/faq). If the price is what's keeping you out, email us and we'll sort it out. The [Youth Access Fund](https://bc-ai.ca/youth-access-fund) pays for scholarships.

> If you change the deal on the people who showed up first, call them yourself. Then make the new deal one you can keep.

## Three hundred is a different job

**[KK: your line on why the next 300 is a different problem from the first 300. Open-facts question 5.]**

Here's what I can see from inside. On the anniversary night, about seventy percent of the hands in the room went up for "been here before." The monthly meetup is still the drumbeat. The work happens in the rooms that grew out of it, and those rooms have people holding them:

- **Kevin Friel** runs the [AI Film Club](https://bc-ai.ca/communities/film-club).
- **Jesi Carson** leads the [AI Ethical Futures Lab](https://bc-ai.ca/communities/futures-lab), the room that [answered Ottawa twice in one year](https://bc-ai.ca/news/the-room-that-wrote-to-ottawa).
- **Matthew Schwartzman** co-organizes [FV+AI](https://bc-ai.ca/communities/fraser-valley-ai) in the Fraser Valley.
- **Anthonia Ogundele** co-leads [Ed + AI](https://bc-ai.ca/communities/ai-education).
- **Lourdes Gant** leads the [Comox Valley](https://bc-ai.ca/communities/comox-valley) chapter.
- **Michelle Diamond** and **Viktor Serbin** keep the network's photo and video memory.

If you're new, find one of those people. They're the reason you'll come back.

Three hundred people who share context, trust and practice are a capability. I [wrote that in July](https://kriskrug.co/2026/07/31/ai-lands-inside-every-profession/), and year two is about keeping it true as the room gets bigger.

Three hundred also buys a voice. At the anniversary meetup I told [Taleeb Noormohamed](https://bc-ai.ca/news/vancouver-ai-meetup-32-data-for-good-taleeb-noormohamed-recap), the MP for Vancouver Granville, that he has an AI think tank in his riding, 300 members strong, and it wants a seat at the table.
