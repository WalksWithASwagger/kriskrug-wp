Brand at a glance

Everything below is read from the live Aurora theme, so a card built from it will match the site rather than drift from it.

Voice

Human, curious, warm, sharp, weird, useful. Explain the tool through people and consequences. Name authorship, labor, trust, and consent directly. Leave the audience with something to do on Monday.

Refuse

Clinical product-demo language, guru posture, corporate cheerleading, contrarian theatre, and weirdness without a point.

Core colour

Surface #efe6d2. Primary ink #171310. Accent #9a2f14. Structure line #d9cdb0.

Supporting colour

Sunk surface #e6dcc2. Secondary ink #3d342c. Muted ink #5c5044.

Type

Space Grotesk for display, DM Sans for body, JetBrains Mono for code. System sans is the fallback in each stack.

Using the marks

Keep the accent for emphasis rather than large fills, and keep display type for headings rather than body copy.
