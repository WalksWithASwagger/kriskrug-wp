Featured keynote

Both Hands Full

The theft is real. The transformation is real. Holding both is where the useful conversation starts.

I use AI every day. I also found roughly 1,800 of my photographs in a major training corpus. A search result tells us something about the dataset. It leaves serious questions about consent and which models used what.

This talk starts with the uncomfortable part and stays there long enough to do something with it. We work through creative labour, taste, dependence on tools, and the choices we still get to make.

  • Name what you refuse to outsource.
  • Make your critique and your curiosity specific.
  • Try the Both/And exercise with the people in your room.
