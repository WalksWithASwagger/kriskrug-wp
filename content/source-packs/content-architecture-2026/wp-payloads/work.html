Community · Monthly since 2024

Vancouver AI Meetup

I started a monthly gathering in my studio for people who wanted to show their work and talk about what AI was doing to it. I still host and curate the program. The public archive keeps the talks, recordings and photo recaps reachable after the room empties.

Film · Interactive web

The Sky Smoked Back

I made a 56-second film with Suzy Easton about one rare Vancouver lightning night. The site pairs it with a storm you can run in your browser and a map for people to share their own memories of that night.
