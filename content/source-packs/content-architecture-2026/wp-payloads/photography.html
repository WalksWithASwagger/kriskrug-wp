Two decades in the room, with a camera.

I didn't watch this century happen — I shot it. The TEDxOilSpill expedition into the Gulf after Deepwater Horizon. The Midway Journey to the most remote island on Earth, choked with our plastic. The 2010 Vancouver Olympics. The Dalai Lama's Peace Summit. A decade of SXSW with Iggy Pop, Jack White, and Billy Bragg in the frame. 144,000+ exposures, all from inside the moment.

This is the consequential cut — the work that mattered, not selfies with famous people. Environmental photojournalism that traveled the world, the cultural and spiritual figures who shaped the conversation, and the music and film I shot on the floor. The full 144,000-frame archive lives on Flickr.
